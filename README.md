# Cutler-O’Neill-Meyer-Woodring — concept redesign

A public, static redesign of [Cutler-O’Neill-Meyer-Woodring Funeral Home & Crematory](https://www.cutleroneill.com/) in Council Bluffs, Iowa.

**Live site:** https://kyleoneill5.github.io/cutler-oneill-redesign/

This is a concept redesign — not the official Cutler-O’Neill-Meyer-Woodring Funeral site. Every name, address, phone number, and service description was copied from their public pages (November 12, 2026) and may change. Photographs are theirs.

Their official site title uses “AI” for the state; this redesign uses the correct “IA.”

Hours, a staff directory, and service prices are not published on their site, so they are omitted here. Walnut Hill Reception Center is listed with the street address they published; their page shows a typo in the ZIP code, so the ZIP is left off.
